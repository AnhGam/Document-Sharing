## Brand & Style
The design system is centered on a "Tactile-Playful" aesthetic that prioritizes physical comfort and joy. It is designed to evoke the feeling of soft, squeezable vinyl toys and plush environments. The target audience includes casual gamers and community-driven users who value expressive, friendly, and low-stress digital environments.

The visual style is a hybrid of **Tactile Skeuomorphism** and **Soft Minimalism**. Surfaces are not merely flat planes but "squishy" objects with perceived volume. Every interaction should feel like pressing a soft button or moving a gummy piece, utilizing "bouncy" motion curves and high-radius rounding to reinforce a safe, inviting atmosphere.

## Colors
This design system uses a "Sun-Drenched" palette. The **Primary Yellow** serves as the high-energy focal point for actions, while **Warm Oranges** provide a supporting glow for hover states and secondary highlights. 

**Soft Creams** replace standard whites to reduce eye strain and maintain a cozy, "organic" feel. **Light Browns** are utilized for typography and iconography instead of pure black to ensure the contrast remains soft and harmonious with the overall warm tone.

## Typography
The typography is designed to look "bubbly." We utilize **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines to take advantage of its rounded terminals and optimistic apertures. For body text, **Be Vietnam Pro** provides a friendly yet highly legible experience that maintains the warm, contemporary character of the system. Text should avoid pure black; instead, use deep warm browns to maintain the soft-contrast aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a **Fluid Grid** model with generous "breathing room." Content is organized into soft panels with wide margins to prevent the UI from feeling cluttered or overwhelming. 

Instead of tight, rigid grids, use a dynamic padding approach that scales with the viewport, ensuring that elements always feel like they are floating comfortably within their containers. Spacing should always be multiples of 8px to maintain a rhythmic but "chunky" feel.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is conveyed through **Tactile Shadowing** and **Soft-Volume tiering**. 
- **Base Level:** Soft Cream backgrounds.
- **Mid Level (Panels):** Raised surfaces using subtle internal glows and soft, diffused drop shadows (color-tinted with a warm brown hue at 10% opacity).
- **High Level (Buttons):** 3D-ish effects created using a 4px bottom-border "offset" to simulate a physical button height. 

When an element is hovered, it should "lift" (shadow grows softer and larger), and when pressed, it should "depress" (the bottom-border offset disappears, and the element scales down slightly to 98%).

## Shapes
The shape language is strictly organic. Sharp corners are forbidden. The default `roundedness` is set to **Level 2**, giving even the smallest elements a friendly, pebble-like quality. Larger containers and cards should utilize `rounded-xl` (1.5rem) to reinforce the "squishy" feel of the overall interface.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Must feature a "3D" base. The primary button is #FFD700 with a darker orange 4px bottom-edge. Text should be bold and centered.
- **Cards:** Soft Cream (#FFF9E5) backgrounds with a 1px solid border in a slightly darker cream, paired with a very soft, tinted ambient shadow.
- **Input Fields:** Recessed appearance using a subtle inner shadow to look "pillowy."
- **Chips/Labels:** Pill-shaped with high-contrast warm brown text on a light orange or cream background.
- **Progress Bars:** Thick, rounded bars with a "glossy" highlight streak running through the center to simulate a candy-like texture.
- **Icons:** Thick stroke weights (2px minimum) with rounded caps and joins, always using the tertiary brown color.
